How to make

Chicken Parmesan Pasta

Dinner will never be the same!


Try out the Recipe

17 ingredients

45 minutes

4-5 servings

This pasta dish is sure to change your dinner routine forever. It's an easy-to-make dish that requires few ingredients, less than an hour of cooking time, and typically feeds a family of five. What makes this dish so special? Its versatility! With caution, feel free to tweak this recipe as desired.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 lbs chicken breast
  • 1 1/2 tsp sweet smoked paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp thyme
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Pasta

  • 12 oz farfalle pasta
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp dry shallots
  • 2 tsp italian seasoning
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups heavy cream
  • 12 oz shredded parmesan
  • 1/4 cup pasta water
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, boil the pasta (box, al dente).
  2. Place all chicken seasonings into a small bowl, mix.
  3. Pound chicken down to thin, then season both sides with mixed seasonings.
  4. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil on medium heat. Once hot, sear the chicken for five minutes on each side. Once both are seared, flip the chicken and lower the heat to medium low. Place the lid on the skillet and cook for an additional 10-12 minutes (temperature at 165 degrees Fahrenheit).
  5. Remove the chicken and place on a cutting board.
  6. Place butter in the skillet and allow it to melt.
  7. Add the pasta seasonings and allow to simmer for 1 minute.
  8. Add the flour, stir well.
  9. Add chicken broth and heavy cream until a creamy sauce forms.
  10. Add parmesan cheese and stir. Allow to cook until sauce reaches the desired consistency.
  11. Add cooked pasta and toss well to coat evenly. Add pasta water one tablespoon at a time to thin out the sauce.
  12. Slice chicken breast and add to pasta. Serve and enjoy!
